# BRIGHT-COFFEE-SHOP-SALE-ANALYSIS

# Purpose
This project focuses on extracting business insights from historical transactional data of Bright Coffee Shop. The goal is to help the new CEO make data-driven decisions to grow revenue and improve product performance.

# Project Overview
The dataset “Bright Coffee Shop Sales” contains daily sales transactions.
As a Junior Data Analyst, my task was to analyze this data using SQL and Excel to identify key trends, top-performing products, and business opportunities.

# Objectives
- Identify products that generate the most revenue
- Find the time of day when the store performs best
- Analyze sales patterns across products and time intervals
- Provide recommendations to increase sales and improve operations

# Tools Used
- Snowflake – Data cleaning, transformation, and analysis
- Excel – Pivot tables, charts, and dashboard creation
- Miro – Data flow and architecture planning
- PowerPoint – Presentation of final insights

# Key Insights & Recommendations
# Performance and Trends
- Top Products: Coffee and Tea are the primary revenue drivers, with Bakery and Drinking Chocolate also performing well. Products like "Branded" and "Packaged Chocolate" generate very little revenue.
- Peak Times: The highest sales for all locations occur during the morning rush, specifically between 7 a.m. and 10 a.m. Weekdays are generally busier than weekends, with Thursday having the highest total sales and Sunday the lowest.
- Location Performance: Hell's Kitchen consistently has the highest sales contribution daily, followed by Astoria, while Lower Manhattan contributes the least.
- Seasonal Peak: The business experiences a strong seasonal peak during the winter months, specifically May and June.

# Strategic Recommendations
- Optimize for Peaks: Implement a dynamic staffing model to increase staff during morning peak hours (7 a.m. - 10 a.m.) and on high-traffic days like Thursday.
- Product Focus: Prioritize inventory and promotion for top-selling items (Coffee and Tea), and consider cross-selling them with Bakery items.
- Re-evaluate Menu: Remove or reformulate underperforming products (e.g., "Branded" items) to free up resources for more popular items.
- Location Strategy: Investigate targeted promotions for Sundays and the Lower Manhattan store location to attract more customers during low-traffic periods.
